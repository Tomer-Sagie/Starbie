# Starbie
made for hackclub half life event!! following the starbie tutorial, with my own twist on it.

Its basicaly a tamagotchi that you can intertact with through haptics, sound, lights, and a screem as well as a motion sensor which is cool.

## BOM
Check out the bom files for that (Due to the limited funding I get from the starter project, I will only be able to manufacture the base starbie withought my additions. However, I published my full design here to show off my work)

## Important: For the actual files that I will build and put into production, check the sub folders in the hardware, firmware, and gerbers folders named prod____, and the prodBOM

## How it works
I pretty much just followed the given tutorial, but I added two leds, a buzzer for sound, a vibrator for haptics, and a light sensor. It all works through the starbie program that I edited to meet my changes. I am especially proud of the patrick pattern I added to the board ( I worked really hard to keep it there even after all of the changes)

Not much else to say lol, check out my half life project page for the full timeline with timelapses and journal entries n stuff.
